# What the Duck: The Duck Adventure Game

## Overview

"What the Duck" is a text-based adventure game built into the conversational UI. Play as Quackers McGee, a devious white duck with a huge appetite, navigating the quirky village of Widdershins-on-the-Wold.

## How to Play

### Starting the Game

Trigger the game with any of these phrases:
- "Let's play a game"
- "Play a game"
- "Start a game"
- "Play game"
- "Game time"

### Gameplay

1. **Read the Story** - Each scene describes Quackers' situation
2. **Choose Your Path** - Click one of the presented options (with arrow icons)
3. **See the Outcome** - Your choice determines what happens next
4. **Continue** - Make more choices until you reach one of three endings

### Game Options Styling

**Interactive Choices:**
- Text on left, arrow icon on right (matches prompt suggestions)
- 8% grey-120 hover overlay
- Cursor-based pulse on click (12% grey-120)
- Smooth staggered entrance animations
- Arrow slides right on hover

### Game Features

**Interactive Choices:**
- Multiple options at each decision point
- Different outcomes based on your choices
- Three unique ending paths

**Story Nodes:**
- 5 main story beats (Intro, Merriweather, Bakery, Seamus, Final Heist)
- Multiple branching paths
- Humor and mischief throughout

**No Wrong Answers:**
- Every path leads to the village square
- Different methods, different results
- All paths are entertaining

## Story Structure

### Act 1: The Hunger
Start at the pond, realize you're hungry, head to the village

### Act 2: The Journey
- **Mrs. Merriweather** - The widow with throat lozenges
- **The Bakery** - Delicious smells and risky plans
- **Seamus** - The village drunk with potential snacks

### Act 3: The Heist
The village market festival - your chance for the ultimate feast

**Three Ending Paths:**
1. **Fireworks Distraction** - Chaos and cinnamon buns
2. **Bomb Threat** - Complete evacuation, total feast
3. **Bull Release** - Share pastries with your new friend

## Choices Matter

### Conservative Choices
- Knocking on doors
- Polite approaches
- Usually result in failure but safe outcomes

### Devious Choices
- Snares and traps
- Pickpocketing
- Creative solutions
- Higher risk, better rewards

### Chaotic Choices
- Mariachi squirrels
- Bull releases
- Bomb threats
- Maximum chaos, maximum payoff

## Replay Value

- **3 different endings** with unique outcomes
- **Multiple choice paths** through each act
- **Varied success rates** depending on choices
- **"Play again" option** at each ending

## Tips

1. **Be Bold** - The devious options usually work better
2. **Think Creatively** - Unconventional choices lead to fun outcomes
3. **Embrace Chaos** - The crazier the plan, the better it works
4. **No Penalties** - Every path is entertaining
5. **Try All Paths** - Play multiple times to see all outcomes

## Technical Notes

**Implementation:**
- Node-based story system
- Branching narrative structure
- Instant choice feedback
- Seamless integration with conversation flow

**Mixing Modes:**
You can switch between game, story, and data modes:
```
"Let's play a game" → Start duck adventure
[Make choices] → Progress through game
"Show my appointments" → Exit game, show data
"Let's play a game" → Start new game from beginning
```
